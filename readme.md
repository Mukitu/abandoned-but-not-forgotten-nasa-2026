## 🌌 Challenge Overview
**Selected Challenge:** *Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars*[cite: 18, 19]

Millions of kilometers away on the Moon and Mars, dozens of pioneering NASA machines sit in silence[cite: 20]. From the **Apollo 15, 16, and 17 Lunar Roving Vehicles** to Martian rovers like **Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, and Perseverance**, these explorers transformed our understanding of the solar system—yet their journeys often remain buried in dense technical archives[cite: 18, 20].

**Abandoned, But Not Forgotten** bridges raw space telemetry with human curiosity by mapping exact landing and final resting sites alongside plain-language mission stories[cite: 20, 22].

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Interactive Moon & Mars Explorer:** Switch seamlessly between lunar and Martian surfaces to explore pinned hardware locations[cite: 18, 19].
- **Mission Telemetry & Longevity Comparison:** Compare planned vs. actual mission lifespans (such as Opportunity operating for nearly 15 years instead of its planned 90 Martian days) and total distances driven[cite: 19, 22].
- **Curated Mission Storytelling:** Accessible timelines, key scientific breakthroughs, and an integrated 240-second narrated documentary experience[cite: 19, 22].
- **Zero-Barrier Educational Access:** Mobile-friendly, fast-loading interface designed for students, educators, and space enthusiasts worldwide without login barriers[cite: 19, 22].
