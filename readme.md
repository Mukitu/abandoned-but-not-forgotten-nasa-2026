## 🌌 Why We Built This

**Selected Challenge:** *Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars*

Millions of kilometers away on the Moon and Mars, dozens of pioneering NASA machines sit in silence. From the **Apollo 15, 16, and 17 Lunar Roving Vehicles** to Martian explorers like **Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, and Perseverance**, these machines reshaped what we know about our solar system. Yet their stories often remain buried inside dense scientific logs that students and everyday learners rarely explore.

We created **Abandoned, But Not Forgotten** to give these silent explorers a voice—turning real NASA landing coordinates, mission timelines, and scientific milestones into a simple, visual, and inspiring journey for anyone curious about space.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Moon & Mars Surface Explorer:** Switch between the Moon and Mars to inspect pinned landing sites and final resting locations of historic missions.
- **Planned vs. Actual Mission Telemetry:** See how these machines defied expectations—such as *Opportunity* surviving for nearly 15 years instead of its planned 90 Martian days—alongside total distances driven and mission highlights.
- **Human-Centered Storytelling:** Plain-language mission narratives paired with an integrated 240-second narrated documentary experience.
- **Zero-Barrier Educational Access:** Fast-loading, mobile-friendly interface designed for students, teachers, and space enthusiasts worldwide without any login or paywall barriers.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
